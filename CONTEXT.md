# Aniseekr

Aniseekr connects anime works, scene references, real-world places, and a user's personal anime activity across discovery and collection experiences.

## Experience language

**Workspace**:
A temporary presentation of the same Aniseekr data, optimized for the user's current intent rather than their identity or skill level.
_Avoid_: Account type, user tier, permanent persona

**Explorer**:
The workspace for discovering anime-related places and content, identifying scenes, planning visits, and using the camera in context.
_Avoid_: Easy mode, beginner mode

**Collector**:
The workspace for maintaining a personal anime library, tracking progress, rating, organizing, and planning from saved activity.
_Avoid_: Pro mode, advanced user

**Seeker**:
The advanced discovery and control capabilities available within Collector, including deeper search, filtering, recommendations, and source selection.
_Avoid_: Third workspace, third persona

**Handoff**:
A transition between Explorer and Collector that preserves the subject being viewed, the user's active intent, and a predictable return path.
_Avoid_: Reset, restart, redirect to home
